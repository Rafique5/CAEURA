What matters most to you

Move a slider and the ranking changes.

Where the numbers come from. Daily climate series from NASA POWER, AG community, 2015 to 2024. Reference evapotranspiration computed with the FAO-56 Penman-Monteith method. Crop coefficients from FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56, Table 12.

What this tool cannot tell you. NASA POWER meteorological data has a resolution of 0.5° by 0.625°, about 55 by 60 km in Mozambique. Every field inside that area gets the same answer. This is district-level guidance, not field-level precision, and the nitrogen and root-depth scores are ranked judgements rather than measurements.

The data was obtained from the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) Langley Research Center (LaRC) Prediction of Worldwide Energy Resource (POWER) Project funded through the NASA Earth Science/Applied Science Program.
